Assumptions

Every number here is a rough default. Change one and the whole meter recalculates.

How this is estimated

Nobody publishes per-request water figures, so this is a model, not a meter reading. Usage is converted to energy, and energy to water at 1.08 mL/Wh — the rate implied by Google’s 2025 disclosure that a median Gemini text prompt uses 0.24 Wh and 0.26 mL, covering both on-site cooling and the water used generating the electricity.

Claude is counted from tokens: about 0.60 mWh per generated token and roughly a tenth of that per prompt token. Weavy is counted from credits, anchored on a measured ~2.9 Wh per generated image at 9 credits. Gemini Pro is counted per request.

Treat totals as an order of magnitude. Real intensity swings more than tenfold with model size, hardware, batch size, and above all datacenter location: an evaporatively cooled site in a hot dry region drinks far more than a cool-climate one. For scale, an average household gets through about 1,100 L in a day — the water cost of AI is a real question, but it is one about datacenter siting and aggregate demand, not about whether you send one more prompt.
